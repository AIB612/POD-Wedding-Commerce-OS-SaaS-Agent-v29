Pod.AI for WooCommerce

WordPress plugin “Pod.AI Chat for WooCommerce” · version 1.2.1

The same Pod.AI shopping assistant and AI sales agent as on Shopify, now as a WooCommerce AI chatbot. It answers from your store’s own data (products, policies, shipping zones, your FAQ) without an AI key, recommends in-stock products with real prices, adds them to the cart, applies your public coupon and links to checkout. Orders and tracking with order number + e-mail; “contact us” becomes a ticket for your team. The shopper always pays in your WooCommerce checkout.

Install

  1. Download the plugin (pod-ai-chat-for-woocommerce.zip) — WordPress.org listing coming soon.
  2. WordPress admin → Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin → Install → Activate (WooCommerce must be active).
  3. Pod.AI Chat → Settings → Connect to Pod.AI. Your site gets its own key; products sync automatically.
  4. Set the ticket e-mail, colour, pages, FAQ and (optional) a public coupon → Save. The chat button appears on your shop.

Full plugin description, data list and changelog: readme.txt.

What it does

RecommendationsOnly in-stock, purchasable products with a price; budget, recipient, “cheaper”, “more like this”.
ActionsAdd to cart (WooCommerce Store API), apply the store’s public coupon, checkout link. Never takes payment.
OrdersStatus and tracking link after order number + billing e-mail match (Shipment Tracking, Advanced Shipment Tracking, YITH, generic meta).
TicketsThe shopper leaves an e-mail → ticket POD-T-… with the transcript to your ticket e-mail; manage status and notes under Pod.AI Chat → Tickets.
AssistantPod.AI Chat → Assistant for your team: “What needs my attention today?”, KPIs, unanswered questions, tickets with reply drafts, teach answers. Every change waits for your Confirm; orders, products and customers are never changed.
LanguagesEnglish, German, French, Italian, Chinese.

AI agent access (MCP)

With “AI agent access” on (default; one checkbox turns it off), AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or Gemini can shop your store through a read-only MCP server and REST API hosted by Pod.AI. They see only in-stock products with current prices — never orders or customers — and can create a prefilled checkout link. The shopper reviews the cart and pays on your store. This works for Pod.AI stores on WooCommerce and Shopify.

<store> is the WooCommerce site key (ws_…, shown under Pod.AI Chat → Settings → AI agents) or the Shopify shop domain (shop.myshopify.com).

Tools: get_store_info, search_products, get_product, create_checkout_link.

curl -s https://podai.swisspro.site/agent/v1/stores/<store>/products?query=wool+scarf
curl -s -X POST https://podai.swisspro.site/agent/v1/stores/<store>/checkout-link \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"items":[{"product_id":"14","quantity":1}]}'
# → { "checkout_url": "https://your-store/?podai_cart=14:1…", "subtotal": 49, "currency": "CHF", … }

Limits: 60 requests / minute per IP and store, 20 checkout links / minute per IP.

Data

Synced: public product data, your policy pages, shipping zones and settings. Orders are never synced; they are read from your store (signed request) only when a shopper gives the order number and e-mail. Disconnecting or uninstalling deletes your store’s data at Pod.AI.

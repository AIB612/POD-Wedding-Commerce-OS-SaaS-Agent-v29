Privacy Policy

Pod.AI — AI customer chat for Shopify stores · Last updated: 9 November 2026 (encryption, backups, access log)

Pod.AI ("the App") is a Shopify app operated by Dropking AI ("we", "us"), under the POD.AI brand. It adds a customer chat to a merchant's online store that answers questions about products, shipping, policies and orders, and passes questions it cannot answer to the merchant's team. This policy explains what data the App accesses, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept and how it is deleted.

For data about their customers, the merchant is the data controller and Dropking AI acts as a processor on the merchant's behalf. For merchant account data (shop domain, settings, access token) Dropking AI is the data controller.

2. Data collected in the chat

  • Chat messages customers type, the App's replies, detected language and intent, and the store page the chat was opened on. Card numbers and similar payment data are detected and never stored.
  • E-mail address and order number when a customer provides them (order lookup or "talk to a person").
  • Handoffs: when the App passes a conversation to the merchant's team, the handoff record holds the reason, a short summary, the customer's messages and their e-mail for the reply.
  • Anonymous storefront events: chat opened, product card clicked, add to cart via a card, and a daily "chat installed" heartbeat. These contain no personal data.

The App does not use first-party tracking cookies of its own. It stores a random chat ID and the time of the last heartbeat in the visitor's browser storage so the conversation can continue across pages. When Google Analytics is enabled, GA may set its own cookies / storage after analytics consent; events are anonymous (no chat content or customer identifiers). We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or combine it across stores.

3. Where data is stored

Data is stored on our application server in Germany (Frankfurt), separated per shop. Collections used: podai_conversations, podai_handoffs, podai_events, podai_settings, podai_shops (the shop's access token, encrypted at rest) and podai_gdpr (a log of privacy requests with hashed customer identifiers).

Encryption at rest. Every Pod.AI record (chat messages, shopper e-mail addresses, order numbers, handoff contents, event data, settings) is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is written to disk, with a key held only in the server's protected environment configuration; shop access tokens are additionally encrypted on their own. Encrypted backups: a daily backup is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before it is written, stored outside any web-accessible directory with owner-only permissions, and kept for 14 days. Access logging: every access to personal data — merchant staff viewing conversations or handoffs, order lookups in the chat, and privacy requests — is recorded in an append-only access log (who, what, when, which record IDs; never the data itself, identifiers only as keyed hashes). The access log is kept for 12 months and the merchant can review it in the App. All traffic uses HTTPS. Requests from the storefront are verified with Shopify's app-proxy signature; admin requests use Shopify session tokens.

5. Retention

  • Conversations and handoffs: deleted automatically 12 months after the last activity (and each shop keeps at most its 1,000 most recent conversations).
  • Anonymous storefront events: deleted after 13 months.
  • Privacy-request log (hashed identifiers only): 3 years, as proof of compliance.
  • When the App is uninstalled, all data of the shop is deleted when Shopify sends the shop/redact request (48 hours after uninstall).

6. Data subject rights and GDPR requests

The App implements Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks:

  • customers/data_request: we record which conversations and handoffs relate to the customer so the merchant can provide them (shown in the App's admin).
  • customers/redact: we delete that customer's conversations, handoffs and related chat events.
  • shop/redact: we delete all data of the shop.

Customers should send access, correction or deletion requests to the store they shopped with. The merchant can also delete individual conversations through these requests. Under the GDPR and the Swiss FADP you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority.

7. Security

AES-256-GCM encryption of all stored App data and of backups, encrypted access tokens, an append-only personal-data access log, HMAC-verified webhooks, signed storefront requests, per-shop data separation, least-privilege read-only scopes, rate limiting, and payment-data and prompt-injection filtering in the chat. Security incidents are handled under our Security & Incident Response Policy (affected merchants are notified without undue delay and within 72 hours). Merchants can rely on our Data Processing Addendum.

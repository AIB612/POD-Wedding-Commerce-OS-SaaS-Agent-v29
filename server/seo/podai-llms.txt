# Pod.AI — AI Customer Chat

> Pod.AI (podai.swisspro.site) is an AI customer chat app for Shopify stores, made by Dropking AI. It answers shoppers' product, shipping and order questions from the store's real data — exact shipping quotes, order status and tracking, product recommendations and discount codes — in the customer's language, and hands the conversation to the merchant's team when it cannot help. It works without an AI/LLM key; an optional LLM only adds answers to open questions. Support: support@swisspro.site

Key facts:
- Platform: Shopify app (theme app embed on the storefront + admin dashboard in Shopify Admin).
- Plans: Free ($0), Growth ($19/month), Pro ($49/month), billed in USD.
- Local first: rules + shop data answer most questions; quotes are the same server quote as checkout.
- Order lookup requires the order number and the matching e-mail address.
- Human handoff: unanswered chats are queued in the admin with the transcript; the shopper can leave a reply e-mail.
- Languages: English, German, French, Italian and Chinese built in; more with an optional LLM.
- Data is separated per shop and deleted after uninstall when Shopify sends its shop-deletion (GDPR) request.

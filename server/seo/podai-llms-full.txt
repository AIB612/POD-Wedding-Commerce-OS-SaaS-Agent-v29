# Pod.AI — AI Customer Chat

> Pod.AI (podai.swisspro.site) is an AI customer chat app for Shopify stores, made by Dropking AI. It answers shoppers' product, shipping and order questions from the store's real data — exact shipping quotes, order status and tracking, product recommendations and discount codes — in the customer's language, and hands the conversation to the merchant's team when it cannot help. It works without an AI/LLM key; an optional LLM only adds answers to open questions. Support: support@swisspro.site

Key facts:
- Platform: Shopify app (theme app embed on the storefront + admin dashboard in Shopify Admin).
- Plans: Free ($0), Growth ($19/month), Pro ($49/month), billed in USD.
- Local first: rules + shop data answer most questions; quotes are the same server quote as checkout.
- Order lookup requires the order number and the matching e-mail address.
- Human handoff: unanswered chats are queued in the admin with the transcript; the shopper can leave a reply e-mail.
- Languages: English, German, French, Italian and Chinese built in; more with an optional LLM.
- Data is separated per shop and deleted after uninstall when Shopify sends its shop-deletion (GDPR) request.

## Overview (from https://podai.swisspro.site/agent.html)

Your shop's AI assistant. Answers from real data. Quotes, orders and product ideas — instantly, in your customer's language.

How it works: local first, AI only when it helps. Message → understand (intent, product, quantity, country) → if confident, answer with local tools and shop data (catalog, quote, orders, tracking, codes, FAQ); otherwise an optional AI model answers open questions (numbers still come from the tools); with no key, the best local answer plus a person.

### Features
- Hero chat: Opens in your homepage image.
- Exact quotes: Same engine as checkout.
- Secure orders: Order no. + e-mail, or nothing.
- Tracking: From our YunExpress · 4PX shipments.
- Recommendations: By occasion and budget.
- Discount codes: Checks codes. Offers optional.
- Human handoff: Queued in Admin with transcript.
- Guardrails: No injection, card data or spam.
- Knowledge: Built-in + your own Q&A.

### Quick answers
- Do I need an AI key? No. Rules + shop data answer; a key only adds open questions.
- Are the prices exact? Yes — the same server quote as checkout.
- Can anyone look up an order? Only with the order number and the matching e-mail.
- What if it can't help? It hands the chat to your team in Admin.
- Which languages? Replies in English, 中文 and Deutsch; also detects French and Italian.

### Install
- Install Pod.AI from the App Store listing (or your test install link).
- Open Apps → Pod.AI in the admin (embedded dashboard).
- Complete the setup checklist on the Overview tab.

### Enable the chat on your storefront
- Go to Online Store → Customize.
- Open App embeds.
- Turn on Pod.AI Chat and Save.
- Optional: set colour, position, title or greeting in the embed settings or in Apps → Pod.AI → Settings.

### FAQ

Do I need an AI / LLM key? — No. Product, shipping, policy and order answers work from your store data without a key. An optional LLM can answer open questions when you turn it on.

Which languages are supported? — English, German, French, Italian and Chinese are built in. More languages work when an LLM is enabled.

How does order lookup work? — Shoppers provide order number and e-mail. The App checks they match, then shows status and tracking when available.

Where do handoffs go? — Apps → Pod.AI → Handoffs. The shopper can leave a reply e-mail; your team answers by e-mail.

Is customer data shared across stores? — No. Data is separated per shop and deleted after uninstall when the platform sends its shop-deletion request. See the Privacy Policy.

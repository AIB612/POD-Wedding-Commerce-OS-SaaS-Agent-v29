# V26 Studio flow refinement

- Selected KIT primary action changes from “In selection” to “Go to design”.
- Left rail “Add products” and “Start designing” actions are larger and easier to reach.
- Entering Studio from the storefront scenario selector now preloads all unique products from that scenario and opens the Design workspace.
- The preferred buyer mode for the chosen scenario is applied on storefront entry when no buyer is explicitly supplied.
- Clicking a product in the left selected-products rail now opens its product detail drawer.
- Product detail drawer now includes a “Go to design” action; it selects the product if necessary, focuses it, and opens Design.
- Existing Design / Pricing / Delivery / Packaging / Review components and styling were otherwise left unchanged.

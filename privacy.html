3. Cookies, localStorage and analytics

Strictly necessary storage: the session, guest and sign-in state cookies and the localStorage cart described above, and the transcript of the chat assistant, which is kept in your browser's sessionStorage only for the current tab and disappears when you close it.

Google Analytics 4: we use Google Analytics, a web analytics service of Google Ireland Limited (Ireland) and Google LLC (USA), with the measurement ID G-GED5TST3DP. Google Analytics sets the cookies "_ga" and "_ga_<ID>" (up to 2 years) to recognise your browser across visits. Google processes this usage data on our behalf; in GA4, IP addresses are not logged or stored. Event data is deleted automatically after the retention period set in our Google Analytics property. You can object at any time by blocking or deleting cookies in your browser or by installing Google's browser add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). More information: policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies.

  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC — Google Analytics (processor), and, only if you choose "Continue with Google", authentication (Google is an independent controller for your Google account).
  • Shopify Inc. / Shopify International Ltd. — only if you choose "Continue with Shop", where offered (independent controller for your Shop account).
  • PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A. (Luxembourg) — payment processing (independent controller).
  • TWINT AG (Switzerland) — payment processing (independent controller).
  • Our print and fulfilment partners in China, and the carriers YunExpress and 4PX — name, delivery address, phone (if given), email and order contents needed to produce, ship and track your order.
  • Twilio SendGrid, Inc. (USA) — sending order, proof, shipping and login-code emails (processor).
  • The couple who published a wedding website — the RSVP replies submitted through that website.
  • Authorities, where we are legally obliged.

We do not sell your personal data. The chat assistant's answers are generated on our own server; chat messages are not passed to third-party AI providers.

5. International transfers

We are based in Switzerland. The European Commission recognises Switzerland as providing an adequate level of data protection (adequacy decision), so data may flow from the EU/EEA to us. Our website and database are hosted in Germany (EU), which Switzerland recognises as providing adequate protection. Some recipients (e.g. Google, Twilio SendGrid, Shopify) process data in the USA. Such transfers are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (as recognised and adapted for Switzerland by the FDPIC). Your items are made and shipped from China: our production partners and our carriers YunExpress and 4PX are based in China, a country without an adequacy decision; we transmit to them only the data needed to produce and deliver your order, because this is necessary to perform the contract with you (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR, Art. 17(1)(b) revFADP). You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards from us.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and correspondingly the revFADP) you have the right to:

  • access your data (Art. 15);
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • erasure (Art. 17);
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • data portability in a structured, machine-readable format (Art. 20);
  • object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) (Art. 21);
  • withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)), where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights, write to support@swisspro.site. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within one month (GDPR) / 30 days (revFADP).

Right to lodge a complaint: you may complain to a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU/EEA member state of your habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR), and in Switzerland to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC / EDÖB), Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern, www.edoeb.admin.ch.

8. Is providing data required?

Using the Site and placing an order requires the data needed to fulfil the contract (name, email, shipping address, payment). Without it we cannot accept or deliver your order. Signing in with Google or Shop is optional; you can check out as a guest. There is no statutory obligation to provide data to us beyond what is needed for the contract.

9. Automated decision-making

We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR / Art. 21 revFADP). Payment providers may carry out their own fraud checks under their own privacy policies.

10. Security

Connections are encrypted via HTTPS, session cookies are HttpOnly and Secure, and stored credentials for connected services are encrypted. No system is completely secure; we will notify you and the authorities of breaches as required by law.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when our processing changes. The current version and its effective date are always published at pod.swisspro.site/privacy.

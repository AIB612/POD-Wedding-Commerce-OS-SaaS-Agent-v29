1. How delivery time is calculated

Every item is printed on demand for you. Your delivery time is production time + carrier transit time, counted in business days (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays). The exact estimate for your address is shown in the cart and at checkout before you pay.

  • Proof first (default for personalised designs): production starts after you approve your proof. We e-mail you a link as soon as it is ready.
  • Approve mockup at checkout: production starts right after payment.

4. Shipping costs

Shipping costs depend on destination, method and weight and are calculated in the cart and at checkout. Prices are shown excl. VAT; VAT is added for your delivery country.

Features

Everything a POD shop gets asked, answered from your own data

01

Customer chat in your hero

Visitors tap Ask Wed.AI next to the logo and the homepage image turns into a chat. No floating bubble covering your products; one tap brings the scene back.

02

Product answers & recommendations

Answers about every product in your catalog with live unit prices and quantity tiers, and suggests matching items by occasion and budget (e.g. favours under CHF 10), each with a Personalise link into the design studio.

03

Shipping quotes = checkout prices

Quotes use the same engine as the cart and checkout: Standard and Express, the visitor's market and currency, VAT mode, free-shipping thresholds and estimated arrival dates (production + proof + carrier days).

04

Order status & tracking

Customers ask “where is my order?” with their order number; the agent asks for the order e-mail before showing anything and gives the same answer for every miss, so orders cannot be guessed. Tracking numbers are matched against your carrier shipments.

05

Discount codes

Checks whether a code is valid. Optionally the agent may offer a single-use sales discount, off by default, with your percentage, validity, one per customer per period and a monthly cap.

06

Human handoff

When a customer asks for a person, mentions a refund, is upset, or the agent fails to answer several times, the chat is handed to your team with the full transcript. The customer is told when to expect a reply; nothing is e-mailed automatically.

07

Multilingual

Detects English, Chinese and German and answers in the same language, following the language picked in the site header. Safety and handoff messages also exist in French and Italian.

08

Guardrails

Refuses prompt-injection attempts, never asks for or stores card numbers or passwords, rate-limits each visitor, never invents prices (all numbers come from your shop data) and only uses customer-safe tools.

09

Knowledge base

Built-in answers for proofs, returns, customs, VAT, payment, samples and more, generated from your live shop settings, plus your own Q&A added in Admin with a search tester.

10

Admin tools

Behind your admin login: overview, handoff queue, conversation log, knowledge base, settings and a data check that flags missing prices, shipping zones or policies before customers notice.

11

KPIs & feedback

13 KPIs with targets: solved without a human, handoff rate, unanswered replies, positive feedback (👍/👎 on every answer), quotes, recommendations, order lookups, guardrail refusals and more, plus the questions it could not answer.

12

Shopify integration

Connect Shopify shops by OAuth from Admin: push products, sync orders into the same order book the agent searches, return tracking on fulfilment, and serve checkout shipping rates from the same quote engine.

13

Works with or without an AI key

Without a model key it runs on a fast rule engine with all tools. Add an OpenAI, xAI (Grok) or DeepSeek key for free-form conversation with tool calling; Anthropic and Gemini can answer open questions.

How a message is handled

Understand

Detects language and intent, and picks out product, quantity, country, budget, order or tracking number.

Check

Guardrails block injection and card data; handoff rules decide whether a person should take over.

Look up

Calls shop tools: catalog, quote, order lookup, tracking, discount codes, knowledge base.

Answer

Replies in the customer's language with product, quote and tracking cards; every answer can be rated.

How to integrate quickly

Live in a few minutes, no code needed on Wed.AI

  1. 1

    Connect your Shopify store (optional)

    In Admin → Shopify, enter your shop domain and approve the app. Products, orders and shipping rates then stay in sync. Selling only on Wed.AI? Skip this step.

  2. 2

    Configure the agent in Admin

    Open Admin → AI 客服 / Chat agent → Settings: switch the chat on, set your human reply time, the handoff threshold, the greeting and (if you want) the sales-discount rules.

  3. 3

    Add your knowledge

    Built-in answers come from your shop settings. Add shop-specific Q&A in the Knowledge tab and use the tester to check which answer a question finds. Run Data check to fix gaps.

  4. 4

    Enable the chat for customers

    On the Wed.AI storefront the Ask Wed.AI button is in the header on every device. Link to it from anywhere with /?ask=1 (e.g. in newsletters or product pages).

  5. 5

    Optional: add an AI model

    Set LLM_PROVIDER and the matching key (e.g. OPENAI_API_KEY, XAI_API_KEY, DEEPSEEK_API_KEY) in the server environment. Without one the rule engine keeps working.

  6. 6

    Watch the KPIs

    The Overview shows automation rate, handoffs and feedback; answer open handoffs from the Handoffs tab and mark them resolved.

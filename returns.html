1. Personalised items — no right of withdrawal

Almost everything we sell is made to your specifications or clearly personalised (names, dates, your uploaded design). Such items are excluded from the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal for consumers in the EU (Art. 16(c) Directive 2011/83/EU). Swiss law provides no general right of withdrawal for online purchases. Please check your proof carefully before you approve it.

3. Defective, damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives defective, damaged in transit, or differs from the proof you approved, we will reprint it or refund you at no cost. Please:

  • contact us within [14] days of delivery at Sherryxuex@Gmail.com,
  • include your order number and photos of the item and the packaging,
  • keep the item and packaging until the claim is settled.

Your statutory warranty rights remain unaffected.

4. What is not a defect

  • Spelling or layout mistakes that were visible in the proof or mockup you approved.
  • Small colour differences between screen and print (screens differ; printing tolerances apply).
  • Low print quality caused by low-resolution artwork you uploaded after we warned you about it.

5. Lost parcels and wrong addresses

If tracking shows no progress for [10] business days, contact us and we will open a carrier investigation and reprint or refund once the parcel is confirmed lost. Parcels returned because of an incorrect or incomplete address can be re-sent at the customer's cost.

6. Cancellations and changes

You can change or cancel an order free of charge until production starts (i.e. until you approve the proof, or right after payment if you approved the mockup at checkout). After that, production costs are incurred and cancellation is no longer possible.

7. Refunds

Refunds are made to the original payment method (TWINT or PayPal) within [10] business days after the claim is accepted.
